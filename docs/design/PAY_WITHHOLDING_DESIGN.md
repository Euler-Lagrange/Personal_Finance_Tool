# Offline payroll withholding — section 4

Implemented November 4, 2026 in release 0.6.0 / schema 41; see [QA](../qa/QA_2026-11-04_pay_tax.md). It implements Jacob's confirmed W-2 payroll-withholding request, not a personal income-tax return or 1099 self-employment calculation. Real paystubs and personal tax elections remain outside development fixtures.

## Verified primary sources

- [IRS Publication 15-T, 2026](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15t), Worksheet 1A and section 1 annual percentage schedules. Modern W-4 status, Step 2 checkbox, annual Step 3 credit, annual Steps 4(a)/(b) and per-period Step 4(c). Use explicit frequency: weekly 52, biweekly 26, semimonthly 24, monthly 12, independently of worked-week count. The unchecked Step 2 annual adjustment is $12,900 MFJ or $8,600 otherwise; checked uses zero. Compute annual adjusted wages, apply the published standard/checkbox schedule, subtract annual credit with a zero floor, divide by frequency, then add extra withholding. Default to cents; expose optional consistent whole-dollar federal withholding rounding. Preserve the published table bases/thresholds rather than reconstructing them from personal-tax brackets.
- [IRS Publication 15, 2026](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15), Social Security/Medicare section: employee SS 6.2% of eligible wages up to $184,500 for this employer; Medicare 1.45% without that cap; Additional Medicare withholding 0.9% of this employer's wages above $200,000, independent of the employee's eventual filing-status liability threshold. [SSA confirms the 2026 wage base and rate](https://www.ssa.gov/OACT/cola/cbb.html).
- [Kentucky 2026 computer formula](https://revenue.ky.gov/Software-Developer/Software%20Development%20Documents/2026%20Withholding%20Tax%20Tables%20-%20Computer%20Formula%2042A003%20%28TCF%29%2810-2025%29%282026%29.pdf), final page: annualize taxable wages, subtract $3,360, floor at zero, apply 3.5%, divide by frequency. The monthly $3,270 example produces $104.65. The printed biweekly $1,500 example contains inconsistent intermediate/final figures: the stated formula gives ($39,000 − $3,360) × 3.5% / 26 = $47.976923…, or $47.98 to cents. Tests should use the formula and explicitly record that discrepancy, not encode the typo.

Rule packages are immutable/year-specific. Initially 2026 is verified; unsupported years produce a visible unavailable tax estimate and retain manual/empirical choices. No automatic remote updates, inferred W-4 elections or AI tax arithmetic. The UI must identify the supported US-resident modern-W-4 ordinary wage method; unsupported payroll treatments use manual estimates.

## Profile and estimate inputs

Profiles are effective-dated, year-specific, explicitly reviewed, with named regimes. Store filing status/elections, federal/KY exemption and extra withholding, independently configured local taxes, benefit/deduction rules and notes. No personal profile is created from the user's hourly rate. Local taxes are user-configured, clearly labeled, with an explicit review even when none apply.

Deduction rules support fixed/percentage amounts, normal-benefit holidays, one-pay-date exceptions, optional annual caps and separate reductions to federal, KY, SS, Medicare and local taxable wages. One percentage base must be explicit; employer contributions must not silently reduce cash pay. A capped deduction needs its prior YTD usage; missing is not zero. Other pay distinguishes taxable cash, noncash taxable benefits and reimbursements, with explicit tax-base inclusion. Unsupported negative/recovery payroll adjustments must not masquerade as a routine positive paycheck estimate.

Each tax prediction retains the selected profile and per-period inputs, including confirmed same-employer SS/Medicare YTD wages before the target payment, capped-deduction/local-tax YTD values, other pay and one-time deductions. A later profile/YTD correction marks changed inputs without rewriting the saved version. Whole-period gross supports rate segments. Use bounded integer cents and rational rates; round each withholding line once and reconcile cash gross, cash deductions, taxes and net. Missing required inputs or negative available cash block a precise result.

## Calibration and history

Create an inspectable tax comparison for a reviewed known paycheck using that paycheck's explicit earlier YTD context. Retain observation/profile/input/calculation snapshots and residual (actual net minus modeled net). Calibration must exclude corrected/stale comparisons, target/future paychecks and incompatible paid-year/rate/frequency/benefits/regime or FICA-threshold phases. Use a small transparent median residual only with adequate earlier support, bounded by an explicit maximum correction. Show raw/applied correction, supporting comparisons and observed spread. Do not blend withholding-year/rate changes automatically or call sparse residual fitting a confidence interval.

New snapshot fields and optional input/result fields must omit empty defaults to preserve schema-40 hashes. Keep existing model-version-1 results replayable; tax/calibrated forecasts use explicit version dispatch. Extend recovery/history/source validation and set a new reader barrier. No bank deposit or cash-plan mutation occurs in this section.

## Implemented choices and review map

[pay_tax.rs](../../src-tauri/src/pay_tax.rs) owns immutable US-KY-2026-v1 rules. Profile and input bounds reject removed-rule references and ambiguous date/year combinations. Capped deductions require prior usage only when applicable; benefit/date holidays do not block an otherwise supported estimate. Percentages use four-decimal fixed-point values. Each tax line rounds half away from zero to cents. W-4 exemption produces zero federal withholding; Kentucky exemption produces zero KY withholding. Local percentage/fixed amounts and caps are explicit configuration, not an automatic jurisdiction lookup.

At least two compatible earlier comparisons are required for calibration; fewer than four shows sparse-support guidance. Duplicate representations cannot inflate support. Compatibility includes the exact retained profile, unchanged known fact, paid year/rate/frequency/benefits/regime, and SS/Additional-Medicare threshold phase. The median actual-minus-modeled residual is clamped to the per-prediction maximum and cannot produce negative net or net above cash gross. Unsupported special pay, mixed rates for calibration, or missing context leaves that method unavailable rather than silently substituting an estimate. The uncalibrated tax method supports daily rate segments.

[PayTax](../../src/PayTax.tsx) edits profiles and shows per-paycheck context/breakdowns; [PayTaxComparison](../../src/PayTaxComparison.tsx) retains historical comparisons; [PayPrediction](../../src/PayPrediction.tsx) selects one method per period. These use native previews and revision/idempotency guards, retain drafts after errors and clear previews after input changes. Saved version 2 predictions include original profile, inputs, tax bases/lines and calibration support; version 1 keeps its original serialized shape/calculation. [Core tests](../../src-tauri/src/tests/pay_tax.rs) cover formulas, edge cases, history, tamper rejection and actual encrypted handoff using synthetic data.
