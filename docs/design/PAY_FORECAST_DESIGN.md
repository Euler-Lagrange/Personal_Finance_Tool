# Known pay and retained forecasts

Section 3 is implemented in release 0.5.0 / schema 40; see [QA](../qa/QA_2026-11-04_pay_forecast.md). This design implements the known-versus-predicted variation workflow from S7 and the confirmed hourly/W-2 requirements. Withholding calculations remain section 4; financial adoption/matching remains section 5.

Known observations are facts supplied manually or reviewed against retained payroll-source evidence. Keep paid date, optional period link, worked hours, whole sick hours, contractual rate, gross, nullable net, optional deduction detail, frequency, benefits pattern and a named deduction/tax regime. Missing net is not zero. Historical aggregate hours do not prove daily holiday/sick compliance. Corrections retain prior revisions. The app never creates a bank deposit from a paystub observation.

Empirical estimates use reviewed, arithmetically consistent observations with the same paid year, rate, frequency, benefits pattern and regime. Exclude the target period and observations on/after its pay date to avoid training on the answer. Exclude special other-pay/reimbursement observations from the ordinary hourly model. Group equal gross points, retain observed spread, and interpolate between neighboring points using integer/rational arithmetic. At least two distinct gross points are required; extrapolation needs an explicit choice and remains labeled low confidence. Display supporting count/range and the limits of a model that does not calculate tax YTD thresholds.

Gross for a period sums exact daily rate/hour products across all assigned weeks before rounding once. Actual hours replace planned hours. An optional, explicit “assume usual week for missing days” setting fills only the estimate, excluding known official holidays; it never writes timesheet actuals. Missing rates and unresolved sick allocations remain review blockers. A manual net estimate with a reason is available when a suitable model is unavailable.

Saving a forecast creates an immutable version with its period, hour/rate inputs, selected method, supporting observation snapshots and result. Selecting a later forecast does not rewrite an earlier one. A freshness comparison exposes changed inputs, while actual-versus-predicted comparison uses the retained earlier result. Forecast creation time comes from native history records; numerical outputs are computed in the native core. Calendar/rate/observation edits cannot silently create income or alter a bank transaction.

Variations presents known observations separately from scenarios for chosen total hours. Ratios with zero/missing denominators stay unavailable. Net per paid hour and incremental take-home for an extra hour have distinct labels. Multiple rates/years/regimes are not blended automatically. The existing payroll revision/history and source-integrity model extends with omitted empty fields so older history hashes remain stable; a schema barrier protects older releases from newer records.

Required QA: exact rounding across weeks, zero/missing values, mixed rates and years, sparse data, interpolation/extrapolation, target/future exclusion, conflicting actual arithmetic, stable saved predictions after corrections, duplicate/retry/stale saves, migration/recovery/handoff and unchanged financial totals. All fixtures remain synthetic.

Implementation detail: the interpolation first rounds each equal-gross group mean to cents, then rounds the neighboring-point interpolation half away from zero. Fit MAE leaves each interior gross point out, interpolates its neighbors and compares all actual nets at the held-out point; edge points are excluded. This local fit statistic is not a temporal backtest or a confidence interval. Structured pay rows retain source locations/quotes and stay unreviewed; arbitrary PDF/image layouts retain originals and extracted text for manual entry.

The schema-40 barrier adds no table. Observation/forecast maps and selection maps append to the existing snapshot with default/omit-empty serialization. Each change is still revision checked, idempotent and replayed from the historical default. Model version 1 calculations must remain stable for history replay; future algorithm changes need explicit version dispatch or a deliberate migration, never a silent rewrite. The original forecast period may remain historical after removal, while current selected forecasts and linked facts must be cleared/unlinked before period removal.
