## Scope

Recurring month-based schedules, stable prepared-month snapshots, expected/confirmed totals, changed-bill view, due-date overrides, cancellation, partial/combined payment allocation, explicit payment recording, and invoice-source links. Bills and evidence never create spending implicitly. Autopay is a reminder. Grouped charge lines are the next section.

## Findings and corrections

- A payment equaling an estimate could appear fully paid before the total was verified. Estimated bills now remain **Verify total**, including on the dashboard; the user can explicitly adopt the paid total and save.
- Attaching evidence or unlinking a payment could close the bill editor and discard unsaved notes/amount changes. Reproduced a failing browser regression, then guarded all related actions until the bill changes are saved. Input stays visible and no mutation is sent.
- Invoice/payment links cannot overallocate either the bill or expense. Financial edits, deletion and import undo require unlinking first. Descriptions/notes remain editable. Refunds do not silently change the obligation itself.
