# Savings goals and funding — November 3, 2026

## Scope and outcome

Schema 19 adds one goal per real savings account, with target amount/date, monthly contribution plan, archive controls and a default budget policy. Goals show the latest known account estimate, retain unknown balances, and list actual contributions, withdrawals, purchases, refunds and explicitly labeled interest. Targets, plans and labels create no cash movement. Changing a default policy affects new assignments only.

Funding can cover part of Jacob's share, multiple charge lines and multiple goals. Valid goal-only funding reduces monthly allowance usage while actual spending, account activity and household allocations remain unchanged. Evidence is an actual savings withdrawal to checking, a goal-account payment to the purchase's card, or direct payment from the goal account. Withdrawals cannot be assigned beyond their actual amount. Unknown, insufficient or unresolved source evidence stays flagged without allowance credit; newly supplied evidence requires an explicit recheck of a previously held assignment.

Refunds cumulatively reverse the original funded proportion in the refund's received month, using integer cents. They do not invent a deposit into savings. Funding changes require removing dependent refunds first. Funded purchases and supporting withdrawals protect their financial fields/deletion/undo, while notes remain editable. Writes are revision-checked, atomic and retry-safe. Account archive, recovery and migration retain history.

## Findings and corrections

- Cross-month regression reproduced a corrected savings checkpoint invalidating original funding without flagging a later refund month. Both months now show provisional funding, and the later month's review opens the original purchase even when it lies outside that month's ledger.
- Background goal refresh previously cleared the loaded list and could unmount an open editor. Refresh now preserves the editor and its unsaved choices; workspace/month changes still isolate views.
- Multiple goal assignments and penny refunds use a combined cumulative personal-share reversal, avoiding per-goal rounding drift. Real cash remains separate from funding designations.
- Integration checks protect a purchase shared by imports, a bill allocation and goal funding. The same event supplies all three workflows; no second spending or account event appears. Old import undo and grouped purchase editing cannot bypass its dependencies.
- A simulated receipt-write failure rolls back all funding rows and the purchase revision; retry commits once. Restoring corrupt funding rejects before replacing the current workspace.
