# Basic category and merchant trends - November 3, 2026

## Scope and result

Annual reports now includes Spending trends: monthly signed spending, calendar-month changes, category or merchant/payee rankings, closed-month averages and contributing transactions. Select a group to inspect its pattern; select a month to narrow the details. Search groups locally. Actual dates and explicit budget periods stay visible. The report is read-only and requires no new schema beyond 23.

Totals are Jacob's confirmed share after refunds, including goal-funded purchases, loan/debt payments and PayPal fees. Income, rewards, card payments, transfers and household settlements are excluded. Refunds use the original merchant in the refund's reporting month. Itemized category shares reconcile without double counting. Missing/future months remain blank, closed zero months count in averages, and open/unresolved activity remains qualified.

Merchant/payee grouping uses current descriptions normalized for case, punctuation and whitespace. Different descriptions stay separate; source identity is never changed. Historical labels can reflect nonfinancial corrections. Monthly amounts reconcile against the authoritative annual report, including saved closed totals. Deeper merchant enrichment remains follow-on work.

## Review and corrections

- Added a native reconciliation check so inconsistent monthly detail cannot silently appear as a valid trend report. Category details use saved exact-cent lines; the PayPal contribution is only its fee, not the bank deposit.
- A first synthetic mixed-ledger test attempted a repayment larger than the payer owed. The existing financial guard correctly rejected it; the fixture now uses a valid partial repayment.
- Scoped CSS uses the existing theme tokens. Compact charts scroll inside their panel, retaining twelve periods without overflowing the desktop window. Negative months extend below the baseline.
- Old results are hidden during loading and on failure. Late requests are discarded after year/workspace changes. Annual totals also no longer render their retained previous result alongside a failed refresh.
- No known correctness, data-loss or workspace-isolation defect remains in this section.
